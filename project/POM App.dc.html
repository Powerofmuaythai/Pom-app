P.O.M Mobile App · Konzept 01

Shopping app, German UI. Browse to bag — checkout is intentionally a dead end. The scanner sits in the centre of the tab bar and does two jobs: scan gear on the shelf in a P.O.M store, or scan the QR on gear you already own to get an authenticity passport, care notes and a reorder path.

Screens
Notes

Gold stays an accent: primary button, price, the 2px active indicator, the scan reticle and the authenticity seal. Nothing else. Every image is a labelled placeholder naming its crop — drop real photography in and the layout is unchanged.

Product detail hides the tab bar for a persistent buy bar. Size guide and filters are bottom sheets. Tap the gold shutter on Scan to simulate a read.
